Throughout this course, I learned a lot about robot control and dynamics. At the end of the semester I had the chance to apply the knowledge I have learned on a fun project. I was tasked to navigate the robot through an obstacle course by planning the trajectories and applying appropriate control laws as well as applying a preset amount of force on an egg without cracking it. I utilized various control techniques such as PID, force, task space, impedance, and feedforward joint control. The code was developed in C and MATLAB was used to calculate Jacobian matrices, plotting data etc.

At the end, I was able to complete the obstacle course smoothly and applying the desired force on the egg without cracking it successfully.
